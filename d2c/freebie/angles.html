10 Creative Angles

Meta Andromeda 2.0 ke baad creative hi targeting hai. Ek ad ke 10 version nahi, 10 alag baatein chahiye: har angle ek alag khareedne wale tak pahunchta hai.

1

Customer ki problem

Pehle dard dikhao, product baad mein. Pehli 2 second mein customer khud ko pehchaane.

Roz subah takiye pe baal?
2

Product in use

Asli haath, asli jagah. 3 second mein samajh aaye ki product kaam kaise karta hai.

Bas 10 second, aur ho gaya.
3

Customer review

Real review ka screenshot ya voice note, customer ki permission ke saath.

Teesra order hai mera.
4

Before / after

Same light, same angle, sach mein jo badla wahi dikhao. Jhootha result mat dikhao.

Pehle dekho, phir 30 din baad.
5

Comparison

Aapka product vs normal option. Competitor ka naam ya logo nahi, bas farak.

Normal bottle vs hamari bottle.
6

Founder story

15 second: ye product kyun banaya. Log brand se pehle insaan se judte hain.

Mujhe khud ye problem thi…
7

Unboxing

Box khulne ka pehla impression: packaging, smell, note, gift. Order ke baad ka feel.

Dekho box mein kya aaya.
8

Myth vs fact

Aapki category ka ek galat belief todo. Seekhne wala viewer ruk ke dekhta hai.

Mehnga matlab accha? Nahi.
9

Price breakdown

₹ kahan jaata hai: material, quality, delivery. Daam ko “worth it” banao.

₹799 mein se kya kya aata hai?
10

Offer

Sirf ek clear offer, saaf deadline ke saath. Kai offers ek saath mat do.

Sirf aaj: 2 lo, 1 free.
